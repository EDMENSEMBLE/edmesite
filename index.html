ⓘ About EDME

the eugene difficult music ensemble (EDME) performs and commissions underrepresented experimental works in order to open ears and minds — in eugene, oregon and beyond. ˖ ݁♬⋆.˚𝄞

⊙ mission

we work to expand the definition of what music can be and what music is capable of achieving, as well as who is capable of achieving it.

| history

EDME started in 2019 as a trio (soon a quartet) performing bizarre music around eugene. pre-COVID, we co-curated new music nights at venues not normally associated with composed music, bridging trained and untrained musicians — in yoga studios, dive bars, game shops, and concert halls alike.

throughout the pandemic we kept going: a YouTube channel that now hosts nearly 30 videos, outdoor performances at open-air studios and ArtCity's BEAM, and a livestreamed Eugene Garbage Project that turned collected trash into a concert-length work for voice and electronics.

since october 2022, our annual New Music Festival has brought guest artists and experimental concert music to downtown eugene — female-identifying, LGBTQ+, and non-white composers are featured strongly among the nearly four dozen composers selected so far. in 2025 we premiered Ambient Ecology, a series of calming, ecologically-inspired concerts at Maurie Jacobs Park, and we plan to keep bringing it back every spring.

✦ New Music Festival

✦ composers from historically underrepresented groups are strongly encouraged to apply — including people of color, and LGBTQIA+ individuals. our review process is not anonymous, since we want to know who our composers are.

our 5th annual festival — presented with IMPULSE New Music Ensemble, our third year partnering together, is a four-concert series of experimental scored and unscored works, improvisations, and audience participation pieces, occuring on October 2–4, 2026 in eugene, or. all performances are free and open to the public.

we welcome works across all experimental traditions — acoustic, electronic, improvised, mixed-media, and beyond. we also accept text-based and graphic scores. no stylistic prerequisites. we prioritize work that takes risks. ꩜
